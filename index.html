MEDICAL KNOWLEDGE

Blood Group Compatibility Guide

Understanding ABO and Rh factor compatibility is vital in emergency transfusions. Explore who you can give blood to and receive from.

O-
🌟 Universal Red Cell Donor
Can Give To: Everyone (All 8 Types)
Can Receive From: O- only
Crucial in trauma & emergency rooms when blood type is unknown.
O+
~38% Population
Can Give To: O+, A+, B+, AB+
Can Receive From: O+, O-
Most requested blood group in hospitals.
A+
~34% Population
Can Give To: A+, AB+
Can Receive From: A+, A-, O+, O-
Second most common blood group globally.
A-
~6% Population
Can Give To: A+, A-, AB+, AB-
Can Receive From: A-, O-
Valuable for both Rh-positive & negative recipients.
B+
~10% Population
Can Give To: B+, AB+
Can Receive From: B+, B-, O+, O-
High demand in South Asian populations.
B-
~2% Population
Can Give To: B+, B-, AB+, AB-
Can Receive From: B-, O-
Rare blood group; donors are frequently contacted.
AB+
👑 Universal Red Cell Recipient
Can Give To: AB+ only
Can Receive From: Everyone (All 8 Types)
Universal plasma donor as well.
AB-
~1% (Rarest)
Can Give To: AB+, AB-
Can Receive From: AB-, A-, B-, O-
Rarest blood type; vital in specialized transfusions.
⚠️
Important Medical Notice:

Blood compatibility and donor eligibility must always be confirmed through laboratory cross-matching by qualified medical professionals and licensed blood banks. Never make clinical transfusion decisions based solely on general compatibility charts.

⚠️ Medical & Platform Disclaimer

BloodLink is a technology platform designed to help connect voluntary blood donors with hospitals and people requesting assistance during emergencies. Blood donation eligibility, blood collection, medical testing, cross-matching, storage, and transfusion must always be handled by qualified medical professionals and authorized blood banks according to applicable laws and medical standards. BloodLink does not store, sell, or collect blood products.
